Hosting

Your own server in a few clicks

Make a Fabric or Vanilla 1.21.11 server on your PC. Friends on your Wi-Fi join straight away, and a free tunnel lets anyone join.

Looks after itself

Starts again if it crashes, backs up the world every time it stops and keeps the newest eight backups.

Says what went wrong

Port in use, the wrong Java, not enough memory or a broken mod, in plain words on the server's card.

Players in one place

Who's online, the whitelist, operators, bans and who played recently, with Op, Kick and Ban buttons.

Server mods

Add Fabric API, Lithium and FerriteCore in one click, or any Modrinth mod, checked for 1.21.11.

Cosmetics

23 cosmetics, free

Wear one hat, one back item and one extra at a time, each in your own colors. Other Valence players see what you wear.

Fair play

Built to be allowed everywhere

Valence has no x-ray, no reach, no autoclicker and no fullbright. When you join a server that bans a mod, Valence pauses that mod for you and turns it back on when you leave.

FAQ

Questions

Is Valence free?

Yes. The client, the launcher, the cosmetics and hosting your own server are all free.

Can I use it on Hypixel and other servers?

Valence only has mods that don't give an unfair advantage, and it pauses a mod on servers that ban it, like Freelook on Hypixel. As with any client, follow each server's rules.

Which Minecraft version does it support?

Java Edition 1.21.11 on Fabric. Bedrock isn't supported.

Is my Microsoft account safe?

You sign in on Microsoft's own page, and your sign-in stays in the launcher on your PC. It's never shared with Valence's servers or put in exported files.

Can other people see my cosmetics and dances?

Other players using Valence see your cosmetics and the Valence mark on your name. Dances only show in your own game, because servers don't send them to other players.

Where do I get help?

Ask in the Valence Discord at discord.gg/HMtx6Gn4Kq. The team and other players answer there.
